Sheet 02 / 05 — About

A developer's spec sheet.

I'm a full-stack developer who likes the unglamorous parts of software the most — the schema that doesn't need a rewrite in six months, the API that fails loudly instead of silently, the layout that survives a font change without falling over.

Over the last few years I've worked across small product teams and freelance contracts, shipping everything from internal dashboards to customer-facing web apps. I care about clean structure as much as clean visuals — the two usually come from the same habit of paying attention.

When I'm not at the keyboard, I'm probably reading about type systems, sketching UI on actual paper, or arguing that tabs and spaces both have their place.
